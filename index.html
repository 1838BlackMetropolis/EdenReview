Elevator pitches · three of ten

Say it in thirty seconds.

The F.U.B.U. pitch

In 1902, Black Philadelphians did something radical: they built their own. For You. By You. Ninety-three thousand souls on Black-owned, Black-controlled sacred ground. That's ours. It still is.

The reinterment pitch

Lebanon. Olive. The great Black burial grounds were forced to close. Only theirs. Even in death, the mistreatment continued. But Eden held them. Eden holds them still.

The time-travel pitch

Sit in the same space as Marian Anderson, Frances Harper, and William Still. Time travel — the past in the present. Come sit with your ancestors. Eden won't let you forget.

Appendix C · Partnership pathway

Test small. Learn fast. Grow what proves itself.

1
Reach out
Open the door
Connect with a partner whose mission already touches Eden. Lead with the story, not the ask.
Eden's move
  • One warm introduction
  • Share "Why Eden Matters"
  • Ask for a conversation, not a commitment
2
Start small
One easy thing together
A single, low-lift joint action with a clear date and end — a tour for their members, a family-history afternoon, a "That's ours" visit.
Eden's move
  • Offer two or three options
  • One event, one date
  • Agree who does what
3
Build the relationship
Trust while you work
The joint action is the relationship-building. Co-plan, show up, and notice how it feels.
Eden's move
  • Co-plan, don't just host
  • Thank them publicly
  • Hold a short, honest debrief
Decision pointWorth continuing?
Yes — go deeperShared values, follow-through, their people showed up.
Not yet — part warmlyKeep the door open; move to the next partner.
4
Deepen
Sustainable work together
Turn one-time into repeatable: an annual program, an ambassador role, or a sponsorship match to one of the four platforms.
Eden's move
  • Propose a recurring rhythm
  • Invite them to be an ambassador
  • Match them to a platform
